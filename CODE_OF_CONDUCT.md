# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussions focused on the project.
Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report unacceptable behavior, contact the maintainer privately through the email address on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/skhedim). Reports are handled confidentially.
